#复合计算字段
- pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_pct: 粒系_嗜酸（依据: pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_myelocyte_pct, pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_metamyelocyte_pct, pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_band_pct, pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_segmented_pct；表达式: pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_myelocyte_pct  +  pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_metamyelocyte_pct  +  pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_band_pct  +  pba.granulocyte_eosinophil_segmented_pct）
  说明: 粒系嗜酸细胞占比
- pba.neutrophil_precursor_cells_pct: 中性粒前体细胞占比（依据: pba.granulocyte_promyelocyte_pct, pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_myelocyte_pct, pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_metamyelocyte_pct；表达式: pba.granulocyte_promyelocyte_pct  +  pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_myelocyte_pct  +  pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_metamyelocyte_pct）
  说明: 中性粒前体细胞占比
- pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_band_plus_segmented_pct: 中性粒细胞杆状和分叶细胞占比（依据: pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_band_pct, pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_segmented_pct；表达式: pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_band_pct  +  pba.granulocyte_neutrophil_segmented_pct）
  说明: 中性粒细胞杆状和分叶细胞占比
- pba.blasts_pct: 骨髓原始细胞占比（依据: pba.granulocyte_myeloblast_pct, pba.erythroid_proerythroblast_pct, pba.erythroid_megaloblast_pct, pba.lymphoid_lymphoblast_pct, pba.monocytic_monoblast_pct, pba.plasma_plasmablast_pct；表达式: pba.granulocyte_myeloblast_pct  +  pba.erythroid_proerythroblast_pct  +  pba.erythroid_megaloblast_pct  +  pba.lymphoid_lymphoblast_pct  +  pba.monocytic_monoblast_pct  +  pba.plasma_plasmablast_pct）

#复合分析字段

  分析: {pba.analysis} \n提取嗜碱性粒细胞是否增多，True/False/None， 未提及就是None

  分析: {pba.analysis}\n提取嗜碱性粒细胞是否明显增多，True/False/None， 未提及就是None
